BEFORE YOU ORDER

Answers before the WhatsApp message.

Final pricing, flavours and policies should be confirmed by Cream & Crumbs before launch.

How early should I order?

Standard orders should be placed at least 4–5 days ahead. Detailed custom cakes may require more time. A date is only secured after the baker confirms availability and payment terms.

Can you recreate a reference image exactly?

Reference images guide the style, colours and mood. Handmade details may vary, and the final design should retain Cream & Crumbs’ own finish rather than being an exact copy.

How is custom cake pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on servings, flavour, height, design complexity, handmade decorations, toppers, flowers, delivery and urgency. Your form summary helps the baker quote accurately.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available to selected areas in KL, Cyberjaya, Putrajaya, Gamuda Cove and Selangor. Availability and fees are checked using your requested date and area.

Is the cake booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form creates an enquiry brief. Your slot is confirmed only after Cream & Crumbs accepts the order and the required payment has been received.
